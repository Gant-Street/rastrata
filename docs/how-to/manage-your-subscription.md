# Manage your subscription

**Settings**, **Billing** shows how the subscription covering this workspace stands and is where its billing owner subscribes. One flat subscription ($19 a month, or $190 a year, which is two months free) covers all businesses you own under your account — up to 10 businesses self-serve (contact us if you own more). People you invite to a workspace (accountants, administrators and members) inherit access and do not need a subscription of their own.

## The trial

Your account gets one 30-day trial, starting when you create your first workspace, with everything on and no card. Creating additional businesses under the same account shares that trial and does not restart it.

After the trial, an account needs an active subscription to keep editing its workspaces. Without one, every workspace you own becomes read-only: everyone with access can still read the books and export all of them.

## Subscribe

1. Open **Settings**, then **Billing** (as the billing owner).
2. Choose **Subscribe — $19/month** or **Subscribe — $190/year (2 months free)**.
3. Pay on Stripe's own page. Rastrata never sees your card.

## Change the card, the plan or cancel

Choose **Manage subscription**. In Stripe's customer portal you can change the card, switch between monthly and yearly, see your invoices, or cancel. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you paid for.
