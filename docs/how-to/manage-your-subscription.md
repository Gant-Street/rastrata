# Manage your subscription

**Settings**, **Billing** says how your workspace stands and is where you subscribe. An owner of the
workspace manages billing.

## The trial

A workspace has a trial of 30 days, with everything on and no card.

After the trial a workspace needs a subscription to be edited. Without one it is read-only: you can still
read your books and export them, and nothing is deleted.

## Subscribe

1. Open **Settings**, then **Billing**.
2. Choose **Subscribe**. The button says the price.
3. Pay on Stripe's own page. Rastrata never sees your card.

## Change the card, the plan or cancel

Choose **Manage subscription**. In Stripe's customer portal you can change the card, switch between
monthly and yearly, see your invoices, or cancel.
