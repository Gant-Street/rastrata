# Invite people to your books

**Settings**, **Members** lists who can open your books and what each of them can do. Everyone signs in
with their own email address and sees only what their role allows.

## What each role can do

| Role | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including who has access. |
| Administrator | Everything in the books. Adds and removes accountants and members. |
| Accountant | Reads, drafts, posts, corrects and closes. |
| Member | Reads and drafts. |

## Invite someone

1. Open **Settings**, then **Members**.
2. Under **Invite someone**, enter their email address and choose a role.
3. Choose **Send invitation**.

They get an email with a link, sign in with that address, and are in. The link works for seven days.
Until they join they are listed under **Waiting to join**, where **Take back** withdraws the invitation.

## Change what someone can do

Choose another role beside their name. The change takes effect at their next request, and there is nothing
for them to do. An owner can change anyone's role; an administrator can change accountants and members.

## Remove someone, or leave

**Remove** takes away their access at once. What they entered stays in the books.

**Leave** takes away your own. An owner or an administrator can invite you again. The only owner of a
workspace cannot leave: make someone else an owner first.
