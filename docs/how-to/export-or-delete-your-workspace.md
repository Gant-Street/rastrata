# Export or delete your workspace

A workspace is one business and its books. Everything in it belongs to the business, and you can take all
of it away at any time. Both are on **Settings**, **Business**, **Export or delete this workspace**.

## Export your books

An owner or an administrator can export the books.

Choose **Export your books**. You get everything in your books as one file you can keep and open without
Rastrata:

- the entries;
- the accounts and balances, as spreadsheets;
- your reconciliations, rules and payees;
- the history of what changed;
- the record that lets another copy of Rastrata rebuild the same books exactly.

The file is made when you ask and is kept for an hour; it is saved to your downloads.

## Delete the workspace

Only an owner can delete the workspace.

Type the workspace's name to confirm, then choose **Delete this workspace**. Deleting closes the books to
everyone at once. For thirty days you can bring them back from the list of your workspaces; after that
they are erased and cannot be brought back. Export your books first if you want to keep them.
