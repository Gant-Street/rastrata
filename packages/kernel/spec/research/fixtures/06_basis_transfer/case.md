# Basis-preserving transfer

Facts: acquire 2 UNIT at 10 USD each in Broker A, then move 1 UNIT to Broker B
without proceeds or change in beneficial ownership.

Invariant: the transfer realizes no gain, creates no new acquisition, and preserves
the moved unit's acquisition identity and 10 USD basis.

Probe: whether transfers are native semantic operations, inferred from paired
postings, or merely two independently annotated inventory changes.
