# Weighted-average cost

Facts: acquire 2 UNIT at 10 USD and 1 UNIT at 16 USD. The pool contains 3 UNIT with
36 USD total basis and a 12 USD weighted-average unit basis. Dispose of 1 UNIT for
20 USD.

Invariant under average-cost policy: the disposal receives 12 USD basis, realizes
8 USD gain, and the remaining pool contains 2 UNIT with 24 USD basis.

Probe: executable average-cost booking, as distinct from an average-price report.
